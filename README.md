# Simple Interest Calculator

## Introduction

This project is a simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest and total amount based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula

Simple Interest:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

- P = Principal amount
- R = Rate of interest
- T = Time period

Total Amount:

Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

## How It Works

The calculator takes three inputs:

1. Principal amount
2. Rate of interest
3. Time period

It then calculates the simple interest using the formula:

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Finally, it displays the simple interest and total amount.

## Example

If:

- Principal = 1000
- Rate = 5%
- Time = 2 years

Then:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = 100

Total Amount = 1000 + 100

Total Amount = 1100
